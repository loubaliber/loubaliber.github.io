About

Physics gave me the questions. Data science gave me the tools to answer them.

I'm a physics postgraduate with expertise in data science and end-to-end machine-learning pipelines, predictive modeling, and data-driven application deployment alongside hands-on experience in classification models, time-series analysis, and scientific computing across international research collaborations.

Biography

My work lives at the intersection of two fields that don't usually share a vocabulary. On one side: data science, feature engineering, and shipping models that real people use. On the other: passive particle tracking under fluorescence microscopy, extracting viscoelastic moduli from the Brownian motion of tracer beads suspended in hydrogel networks. My MSc thesis at the University of San Carlos characterizes the gelation mechanism of FITC-labeled mixed kappa/lambda carrageenan gels.
