Questions

Before your first session.

How do online sessions work?

We meet on Google Meet or Zoom, and I write on a shared digital whiteboard. You only need a laptop or phone, and a camera helps if you want to show your work.

Can you help me cram before an exam?

Yes. Tell me the exam date and topics, and we'll focus on the problems most likely to come up.

I'm not a student. Can I still book?

Of course. Board-exam reviewees, working engineers and anyone curious about the math are welcome.

Pwede ba mag-Bisaya?

Oo, pwede ta mag-Bisaya, Tagalog o English sa atong sessions.
