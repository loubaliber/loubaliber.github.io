2025

Satellite Launch & Orbit Simulator

A 2D physics simulation modeling a satellite's trajectory from launch through orbital insertion at low Earth orbit. Solves the equations of motion via custom Runge-Kutta 4th-order (RK4) integration under gravitational and atmospheric drag forces, accounting for fuel consumption and mass loss. Trajectory, velocity, and altitude profiles are visualized in real time with Matplotlib animation and validated against analytical orbital-mechanics solutions.
