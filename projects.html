A desktop app that listens to short spoken/vocal signals through your microphone, fingerprints them using audio features (FFT + MFCC), and matches them against a library of previously learned signals — letting you teach it custom "words" and have it recognize them again later. (Similar to Project Hail Mary's Rocky translator.)
