Questions

Before you book.

Will you write my thesis for me?

No. You stay the author, and I coach, review and analyze. That's what your adviser and panel expect, and it means you can answer every question at your defense.

Can you follow my school's format?

Yes. Send me your department's template or guidelines, and I'll check every chapter against them, APA or IEEE included.

Do you help with studies outside physics?

Yes, for statistics, editing and defense prep in most fields. For lab methods, I'm strongest in physics, biophysics, materials and engineering.

Pwede ba mag-Bisaya?

Oo, pwede ta mag-Bisaya, Tagalog o English sa atong sessions.

How fast can you turn a chapter around?

Usually within 3 to 5 days. Rush work before a deadline is possible, so ask on the free call.
